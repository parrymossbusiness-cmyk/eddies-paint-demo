How We Started

Built on Showing Up and Doing It Right

Eddie's Painting has been helping neighbors throughout Simi Valley transform their spaces for more than 15 years. What started as one painter with a truck and a reputation for actually returning phone calls has grown into a full team — but the standard hasn't changed: quote it honestly, show up when we say we will, and don't leave until it's right.

Whether it's a fresh coat of paint inside a home, exterior work to boost curb appeal, or drywall repair and finishing touches before the paint ever goes on, our skilled, licensed, and insured team handles the whole job — paint and stain, wall texture, wallpaper removal, carpentry fixes, and flooring repairs. No job is too big or too small, and we're always happy to answer questions or offer advice before you commit to anything.

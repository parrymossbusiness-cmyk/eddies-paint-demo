The Eddie's Painting Approach

Fixes That Come Up Along the Way, Handled on the Spot

Painting projects have a way of surfacing small carpentry and flooring problems — a soft spot in a doorframe, a section of trim that needs replacing, flooring damaged by an old leak. Rather than sending you off to find another contractor, we handle those fixes ourselves as part of the same job.

That includes trim and molding repair or replacement, door and frame fixes, minor structural carpentry, and flooring repairs tied to water damage or general wear. If it's part of getting a room fully finished — not just painted — we can usually take care of it.

  • Trim, baseboard, and molding repair or replacement
  • Door and door frame repair
  • Minor structural and finish carpentry
  • Flooring repair, including water-damaged sections
  • Can be paired with a paint job or booked standalone
  • One crew, one estimate, one point of contact
How It Works

Our Carpentry & Flooring Process

Assessment & Estimate

We take a look at the damage or fix needed and give you a written estimate before starting.

Repair or Replace

Trim, framing, or flooring is repaired or replaced to match the surrounding material as closely as possible.

Finish Work

Repaired areas are sanded, filled, and prepped so they're ready for paint or stain if needed.

Final Check

We walk the repair with you before considering the job done.
